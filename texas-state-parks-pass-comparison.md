## Other Annual-Pass Benefits

The cost totals above count park entry and the estimated second-night campsite discount only. The annual pass may provide additional value that is not included in the comparison:

- **Park store:** 10% off most retail and rental items at Texas state park stores. Some partnership-operated stores may not honor the discount.
- **Programs and events:** Some parks offer passholder discounts for programs, activities, and events; offers vary by park.
- **Camping:** Half-price second night for eligible consecutive-night stays in the same campsite or regular screen shelter, subject to blackout dates and restrictions. The spreadsheet estimate assumes all four Palo Duro and Tyler campsite bookings qualify.

These benefits could make annual passes more valuable if the group uses them, but their value depends on purchases, activities, and campground eligibility.
