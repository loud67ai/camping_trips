Route Notes

MERUS 101 Green 3 · Beginner

4.5 miles · about 40 minutes

Runs most of the park's length, beginning with the Drop-In into the canyon and crossing Salt Fork Creek several times. Expect short ledges, steep short climbs and drops, and tight rocky steps.

Consider: The Drop-In is a 21-degree rock, sand, and gravel descent. Rain can make the route slick and crossings wet or muddy.

Connects to: Deep Creek, Northbound, Salt Fork Sections 1 and 2, Middle Earth, Huckleberry, Sagissor Blue and Black, 101 Loop Back, Carnage, and Back 40.

Landry's Landing Green 3 · Beginner

1 mile · about 15 minutes

Canyon-access descent from Camp Point toward MERUS 101 and the Drop In ridge, ending at an old cattle gate. The route sheet described it as one-way into the canyon while under construction.

Consider: Drops up to 17 degrees and sharp turns, with soft trail edges and brush near the creek bed. Stay centered on the route.

Access: Area 170 campsite on the south side of Camp Point Trail. No side trips listed; a canyon overlook follows the first descent.

Northbound Green 3 · Beginner

1.5 miles · about 30 minutes

Scenic route through creek beds beside the canyon walls toward the back of the canyon. The guide highlights native wildflowers, grasses, yucca, prickly pear, mesquite, cottonwood, western soapberry, and hackberry.

Consider: Generally easy but requires 4-low; includes creek crossings, a few rocky climbs, and descents. Crossings are usually dry unless it is actively raining.

Access and connections: MERUS 101 near an open old gate; also accessible from Salt Fork Section 1, Tin Tin, and Kate the Great. The trail crosses Salt Fork Section 1 and ends at shaded Meadows Campground.

Roundabout Green 3 · Beginner

0.5 mile · about 10 minutes

Scenic, tree-lined connector between Northbound and Tin Tin, with a creek crossing and panoramic canyon views. The guide describes it as a beginner route suitable for a novice in a stock vehicle.

Consider: A few descents and the creek crossing may challenge long-wheelbase vehicles with poor departure angles. The Tin Tin entrance/exit has soft sand that can be difficult to climb when dry.

Access: From Tin Tin or Northbound. No side trips are listed; the guide calls it a good short-break route.

Spicy Green 3 · Beginner

1 mile · about 30 minutes

Starts on MERUS 101 and climbs toward the Boulders hike trailhead. Turning south from the Boulders, it follows arroyos along the park's eastern boundary with quick, tight turns. The route sheet recommends north-to-south travel because afternoon sun can be blinding.

Consider: Still rated Green, but the tight turns demand attention and offer beginners practice before moving to harder Blue trails. The guide describes it as a shoulder workout.

Access and side trip: Starts and finishes on MERUS 101; the Boulders hike is an optional stop with views from the top.

View Spicy photos

Sidewinder Blue 5 · Intermediate

1.54 miles · about 35 minutes

Runs along the canyon rim. From Park Road West it winds through mesquite brush with a mogul, tight turns, dips, and built-up hills; near MERUS 101 it descends steeply before a sharp turn into an incline with a two-foot wall.

Consider: Tight turns and moguls challenge suspension. Longer-wheelbase vehicles may need three-point turns; assess the final descent and rock wall for your vehicle and experience. The guide says the route may also be run in reverse.

Access: Left off Park Road West just past the V-notch, or begin from MERUS 101 in reverse.

Deep Creek Blue 4 · Intermediate

1 mile · about 20 minutes

Narrow, rocky trail through a canyon riverbed with red sandstone and white Satin Spar Gypsum veins. The guide also notes fragile cryptobiotic living soil across the ground and rocks.

Consider: Frequent small-to-medium rocks and narrow passages. Careful line choice and tire placement help avoid high-centering or catching differentials or suspension components.

Connects: Enter from MERUS 101 and exit onto Salt Fork Section 2. No side trips are listed.

View Deep Creek photos

The Chute Blue 4 · Intermediate

1 mile · about 15 minutes

One-way descent into the north end of the canyon through two rock walls, followed by sharp turns to the canyon floor and a creek-bed run to Tin Tin.

Consider: Starts with an immediate 22-degree descent. The route sheet describes soft dirt and soft edges; stay centered. It notes steep descents but no significant obstacles.

Access and connections: East side of Festival Hilltop camping area near the park entrance. Connects with Tin Tin near Kate the Great.

View The Chute photos

Salt Fork Section 2 Blue 5 · Intermediate

0.38 mile · about 15 minutes

Short creek-bed route along the historic Salt Fork, with medium rocks, multiple lines, and a dry waterfall feature. It provides access to Lonesome Dove and Salt Fork Section 3.

Consider: Line choices range from easy to difficult. Rocks, sand, and red clay make tire placement important; the source guide warns the creek bed can be dangerous in rain.

Access: MERUS 101, Deep Creek, Lonesome Dove, and Salt Fork Sections 1 and 3.
