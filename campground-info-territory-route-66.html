Christmas Day Confirmation

The campground confirmed by email on Sep 28, 2026 that a late arrival on Dec 25 with both reserved sites is okay. Office hours may differ on Christmas Day, so follow the after-hours pamphlet and park-map instructions if the office is closed.

Recommended Route from I-40

1
Take I-40 Exit 101 for Hinton.
2
Travel north about 0.2 mile.
3
Follow the left fork toward Route 66.
4
Continue west on Route 66 about 1.7 miles. The park is on the right, north side of the road.

GPS caution: The campground warns that GPS may not be accurate. Exit 101 is the recommended approach. If the route or entrance is unclear, call the park rather than attempting a turnaround on rural Route 66.

Finding Sites 9 and 10

  1. These are back-in sites, not the Route 66 pull-through sites.
  2. Enter through the main park entrance. During office/check-in hours, stop at the office for the arrival pamphlet and site map.
  3. Use the posted site map to locate Sites 9 and 10 in the rear row. The campground says back-in guests can reach their sites from inside the park; they do not need to exit and re-enter.
  4. If the office is closed, the campground says an arrival pamphlet is left on the clipboard beside the office door. Use the site map posted at the office.
  5. Call (405) 542-6566 for help finding or backing into either site.

The special one-way entry procedure applies to pull-through Sites 14-18, not Sites 9 and 10.

Arrival and Campground Notes

  • The confirmation message lists office/check-in windows as 8:00-9:00 AM and 2:00-6:00 PM. Hours may vary on Christmas; call ahead.
  • Bathrooms, showers, and laundry are inside the office building. Follow private access instructions from the campground confirmation or staff.
  • Ice is available from the office for $2.50 per bag.
  • Keep dogs leashed outside the campers and pick up after them.
  • Gas fire pits are allowed, confirmed by the campground on Sep 28, 2026. Burn bans may still restrict open wood or charcoal fires; ask staff before using those. Any approved campground fire pit must be on gravel and completely extinguished when finished.
  • For site help, late arrival, or other assistance, call (405) 542-6566.

Private booking links, confirmation numbers, office access codes, and Wi-Fi credentials are intentionally not included on this public-facing page. Use the private reservation confirmation for those details.

Cancellation Policy in the Confirmation

  • Cancellation at least 48 hours before check-in: 90% refund.
  • Cancellation 24-48 hours before check-in: 50% refund.
  • Cancellation within 24 hours of check-in or after check-in: non-refundable.

Verify the current policy with the campground before making a cancellation decision.
