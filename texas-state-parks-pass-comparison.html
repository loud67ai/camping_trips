The comparison assumes two families, two adults per family, and one separate vehicle per family.

Camping and park entry are separate charges. Campsite fees are due in addition to park entrance fees. Entry must also be paid per visiting vehicle/day unless a valid annual pass covers that vehicle. An annual pass waives eligible entry fees, not camping, activity, or other fees.

Other Annual-Pass Benefits

The comparison totals count park entry and the estimated second-night camping discount only. Other annual-pass benefits may add value, but are not included in the $302 estimate:

  • 10% off most park-store retail and rentals. Some partnership-operated stores may not honor the discount.
  • Possible discounts on programs, activities, and events. Offers vary by park.
  • Second-night camping discount. Half price on an eligible second consecutive night in the same campsite or regular screen shelter; blackout dates and other restrictions apply. The comparison assumes all four booked sites qualify.

Actual value depends on what the group buys or attends and whether each campground reservation qualifies.

  • Check Tyler State Park day-entry requirements for Dec 29 and Dec 30; its $6 daily rate is the supplied estimate.
  • Annual-pass camping benefit: 50% off the second night for two or more consecutive nights at the same campsite or regular screen shelter. Restrictions and blackout dates apply.
