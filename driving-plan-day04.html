Stop and campsite checks

At 201 N 23rd St, fill both tow vehicles at United Express, then shop at United Supermarkets for dinner, breakfast, water, ice, and dog supplies. They share an address, but confirm pump clearance, trailer parking, gasoline availability, and Dec 28 store hours before relying on the stop. With 12 mpg towing, about 60 road miles use roughly 5 gallons per vehicle before trail driving; start with enough reserve for MERUS and the following day's long drive. If fuel access with trailers is tight, choose another verified, trailer-accessible Canyon station before leaving town.

MERUS Site 311 at East Rise Camp is confirmed for Dec 28–29, with two daily 4x4 passes and four adult entry passes on the invoice. Open Site 311 coordinates and open the park map, but use the park's instructions for the entrance-to-site route; do not follow an unverified GPS shortcut with a camper. Confirm 4WD access, whether both campers can reach the site, water, and the purchased passes with MERUS before leaving Palo Duro. Site 311 has no electrical hookup; generators must be off by 10 PM.

At MERUS

Set up the campers and eat a picnic lunch from 12:45–1:30 PM. Plan approved overlanding from 1:30–4:00 PM, return to the campsite by 4:45 PM, and finish the water, power and dinner setup while there is light. Dinner at the MERUS campsite is planned for about 6:30 PM. Keep the off-road outing flexible if check-in or the Canyon stop takes longer; compare MERUS trails before selecting a route.
